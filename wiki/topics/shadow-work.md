### src-f0c456f9c389-c01

### src-f0c456f9c389-c03

For Swaruu, a shadow is resisted behavior conflicting with self-concept; programming alone is not necessarily a shadow. She says recognizing the resistance begins its resolution.

### src-f0c456f9c389-c04
