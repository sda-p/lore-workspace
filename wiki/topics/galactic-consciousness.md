# Galactic consciousness

Type: cosmology

Aliases: galaxies as conscious beings

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-c3edb79b5f6c-c01

Yazhi describes each galaxy as a conscious being, with its central supermassive black hole as an attention center and its stars and planets as cells. She presents the body-and-cells comparison as explanatory metaphor.

Attributed to **Yazhi**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [LAS GALAXIAS QUE CHOCAN - Yazhi Swaruu](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/las-galaxias-que-chocan-yazhi-swaruu) (2022-12-16; es); passages p0007, p0008, p0010, p0014. [Structured record](../../records/src-c3edb79b5f6c.json).

Related topics: [Black holes](black-holes.md).

### src-c3edb79b5f6c-c02

Yazhi says galaxy collisions combine their energy to form larger galaxies, describing the process as reproduction rather than destruction. She says the process is extremely slow from Earth's perspective but rapid for the galaxy.

Attributed to **Yazhi**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [LAS GALAXIAS QUE CHOCAN - Yazhi Swaruu](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/las-galaxias-que-chocan-yazhi-swaruu) (2022-12-16; es); passages p0029, p0031, p0037, p0038. [Structured record](../../records/src-c3edb79b5f6c.json).

Related topics: [Consciousness and metaphysics](consciousness-metaphysics.md).
