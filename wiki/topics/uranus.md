# Uranus and its moons

Type: planetary system

Aliases: Uranus, Miranda, Titania, Umbriel, Ariel, Oberon

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-d8bcaf4fc008-c01

Swaruu says Miranda has under-ice flora and fauna, fed by geothermal springs and liquid-water pockets, but no intelligent life or bases. Her account.

Attributed to **Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [LOS SECRETOS DEL PLANETA URANO Y SUS LUNAS](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/los-secretos-del-planeta-urano-y-sus-lunas) (2019-06-20; es); passages p0005, p0007, p0009, p0010. [Structured record](../../records/src-d8bcaf4fc008.json).
