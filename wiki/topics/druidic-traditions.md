## Collected claims

### src-4a19348a3734-c01

Athena describes druids as sages, healers and record-keepers bridging physical and spirit worlds through herbal medicine, history and metaphysics. She presents this as her own description.

Attributed to **Swaruu X (Athena)**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [EL MAGO MERLIN EL ULTIMO DRUIDA - EL MUNDO DE LOS ESPIRITUS - ATHENA SWARUU - X](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/el-mago-merlin-el-ultimo-druida-el-mundo-de-los-espiritus-athena-swaruu-x) (2021-08-27; es); passages p0034, p0035, p0037, p0038.
