# Solatians

Type: species

Aliases: white Pleiadians

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-555d02ebcd4e-c01

Solatians inhabit three worlds orbiting Pleione and are Federation members represented through Alcyone Council, with a civilian Earth-orbit presence.

Attributed to **Mari Swa**; reported; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [The Solatian People (English)](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/the-solatian-people-english) (2024-05-06; en); passages p0003, p0004. [Structured record](../../records/src-555d02ebcd4e.json).

Related topics: [Alcyone Council](alcyone-council.md), [Galactic Federation](galactic-federation.md).

### src-555d02ebcd4e-c02

Mari calls them white Pleiadians, distinct from Malakak Tall Whites, typically tall, slender, and pale.

Attributed to **Mari Swa**; reported; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [The Solatian People (English)](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/the-solatian-people-english) (2024-05-06; en); passages p0005, p0006. [Structured record](../../records/src-555d02ebcd4e.json).

Source: [The Solatian People (English)](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/the-solatian-people-english) (2024-05-06; en); passages p0009, p0010, p0011, p0012. [Structured record](../../records/src-555d02ebcd4e.json).
