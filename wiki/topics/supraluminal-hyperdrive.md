# Supraluminal frequency hyperdrive

Type: technology

Aliases: Hyperdrive, Ether jumps

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-ea07e710cf87-c02

He describes plasma engines forming a closed magnetic toroid whose controlled frequency matches the destination for supraluminal travel, without warping spacetime. His account distinguishes it from conventional warp travel.

Attributed to **Dhor Káal’el**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [EXTRATERRESTRE EXPLICA EL PROCEDIMIENTO DE VUELO DE SU NAVE -Dhor Káal'el](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/extraterrestre-explica-el-procedimiento-de-vuelo-de-su-nave-dhor-kaal-el) (2022-05-18; es); passages p0037, p0039, p0043, p0045.
