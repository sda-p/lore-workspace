### src-8ebb49ce3ca1-c01

Mari said karma can mean cause and effect, but she defined “living in karma” as obeying imposed rules in fear and guilt, surrendering personal judgment.

Attributed to **Mari Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Reflexiones sobre Karma y Dharma](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/reflexiones-sobre-karma-y-dharma) (2023-03-14; es); passages p0005, p0006, p0011, p0014. [Structured record](../../records/src-8ebb49ce3ca1.json).

### src-8ebb49ce3ca1-c02

### src-d0e9644dbc32-c01

Mari defines karma narrowly as action’s consequences and broadly as accumulated life and past-life experience shaping a unique soul. She says the broader meaning makes karma inescapable.

Attributed to **Mari Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [¿Evitar acumular Karma, o construir tanto Karma como se pueda en la vida?](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/evitar-acumular-karma-o-construir-tanto-karma-como-se-pueda-en-la-vida) (2023-10-06; es); passages p0004, p0011, p0012, p0013. [Structured record](../../records/src-d0e9644dbc32.json).
