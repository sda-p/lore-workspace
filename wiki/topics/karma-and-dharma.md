### src-8ebb49ce3ca1-c01

Mari said karma can mean cause and effect, but she defined “living in karma” as obeying imposed rules in fear and guilt, surrendering personal judgment.

Attributed to **Mari Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Reflexiones sobre Karma y Dharma](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/reflexiones-sobre-karma-y-dharma) (2023-03-14; es); passages p0005, p0006, p0011, p0014. [Structured record](../../records/src-8ebb49ce3ca1.json).
