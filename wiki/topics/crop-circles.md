# Crop circles

Type: phenomenon

Aliases: agro glyphs, crop formations

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-7027fa794a95-c01

Swaruu says crop circles can be human hoaxes, spacecraft time-space markers, DNA activators, or extraterrestrial communications. She presents multiple possible purposes.

Attributed to **Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Preguntas Constantes que Recibe Telepaticamente Swaruu de Erra (Taygeta, Pleyades)](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/preguntas-constantes-que-recibe-telepaticamente-swaruu-de-erra-taygeta-pleyades) (2019-06-23; es); passages p0012, p0013, p0014, p0015, p0016. [Structured record](../../records/src-7027fa794a95.json).

Related topics: [Stellar navigation](stellar-navigation.md).
