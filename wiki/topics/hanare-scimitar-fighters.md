# Hanáre / Scimitar fighters

Type: spacecraft class

Aliases: Hanáre, Scimitar A, Scimitar B

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-97c055d470ab-c01

Athena described Hanáre/Scimitar fighters as A interceptors optimized for speed and B assault craft carrying troops and up to two armored vehicles. A variants are 50–55 meters long; B variants are 60 meters.

Attributed to **Swaruu X (Athena)**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Vida Interestelar 3 - Scimitars - NAVES DE CAZA - Athena Swaruu](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/vida-interestelar-3-scimitars-naves-de-caza-athena-swaruu) (2023-07-04; es); passages p0009, p0014, p0017, p0019, p0020, p0023. [Structured record](../../records/src-97c055d470ab.json).

Related topics: [Starship systems](starship-systems.md).

### src-97c055d470ab-c02

She said Scimitars use toroidal high-energy stealth to evade human visible-light, infrared, and microwave detection; gravitational-shift sensors could detect them. She said the Cabal lacked the latter technology.

Attributed to **Swaruu X (Athena)**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Vida Interestelar 3 - Scimitars - NAVES DE CAZA - Athena Swaruu](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/vida-interestelar-3-scimitars-naves-de-caza-athena-swaruu) (2023-07-04; es); passages p0032, p0033, p0034, p0035. [Structured record](../../records/src-97c055d470ab.json).

### src-97c055d470ab-c03
