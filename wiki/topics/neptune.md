# Neptune and Triton

Type: planet and moon

Aliases: Triton

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-ae20e2d2296b-c01

Swaruu says Triton hosts extraterrestrial animals, including intraterrestrial life using its geological activity for energy, light, and heat. She says only animals live there.

Attributed to **Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [LOS SECRETOS DEL PLANETA NEPTUNO SUS LUNAS Y EL ASTEROIDE CERES ANTIGUA LUNA DE TIAMAT](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/los-secretos-del-planeta-neptuno-sus-lunas-y-el-asteroide-ceres-antigua-luna-de-tiamat) (2019-06-22; es); passages p0006, p0008, p0010. [Structured record](../../records/src-ae20e2d2296b.json).

Related topics: [Intraterrestrial civilizations](intraterrestrial-civilizations.md).

### src-ae20e2d2296b-c02

By her account, Andromedans towed natural Triton after Tiamat's war into reverse orbit to counterbalance and stabilize Neptune. She cites her data.

Attributed to **Swaruu**; reported; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [LOS SECRETOS DEL PLANETA NEPTUNO SUS LUNAS Y EL ASTEROIDE CERES ANTIGUA LUNA DE TIAMAT](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/los-secretos-del-planeta-neptuno-sus-lunas-y-el-asteroide-ceres-antigua-luna-de-tiamat) (2019-06-22; es); passages p0008, p0012, p0014. [Structured record](../../records/src-ae20e2d2296b.json).

Related topics: [Tiamat](tiamat.md), [Andromeda Council](andromeda-council.md).

### src-6338db41254a-c01

Swaruu (9) says Triton’s subsurface life draws on geothermal energy; Andromedans allegedly towed it into retrograde orbit as a Neptune counterweight.

Attributed to **Swaruu (9)**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Neptuno, Ceres, y mas - Sistema Solar - Swaruu de Erra](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/neptuno-ceres-y-mas-sistema-solar-swaruu-de-erra) (2023-01-25; es); passages p0010, p0014, p0018. [Structured record](../../records/src-6338db41254a.json).
