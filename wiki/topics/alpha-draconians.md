### src-84a31dbc8140-c01

Source: [RAZAS EXTRATERRESTRES en 5D (3) - ALPHA DRACONIANOS (Mensaje Pleyadiano)](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/razas-extraterrestres-en-5d-3-alpha-draconianos-mensaje-pleyadiano) (2019-06-07; es); passages p0002, p0003. [Structured record](../../records/src-84a31dbc8140.json).
