# Taygetan parthenogenesis

Type: reproductive mechanism

Aliases: auto-pregnancy, self-pregnancy, parthenogenesis

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-1d479f08f57b-c01

Swaruu says Taygetan women reproduce sexually, store sperm for decades, or trigger parthenogenesis: an egg completes its DNA, producing a female clone sharing her consciousness. She says this occurs mostly in virgins under severe loneliness or stress.

Attributed to **Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [AUTOEMBARAZO - PARTENOGÉNESIS- SWARUU- Sistema Taygeta - LAS PLEYADES](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/autoembarazo-partenogenesis-swaruu-sistema-taygeta-las-pleyades) (2018-07-15; es); passages p0013, p0014, p0015, p0016, p0017, p0019. [Structured record](../../records/src-1d479f08f57b.json).
