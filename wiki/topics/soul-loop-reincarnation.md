# Soul-loop reincarnation

Type: cosmology

Aliases: soul loops, reincarnation loops

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-3cfcc923505e-c01

Yazhi describes soul loops as returning to earlier lives through attachment and frequency compatibility; the sequence need not follow linear time. She frames this as her metaphysical model.

Attributed to **Yazhi**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Reencarnación - Bucles de Alma - Información Extraterrestre de Yazhi Swaruu](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/reencarnacion-bucles-de-alma-informacion-extraterrestre-de-yazhi-swaruu) (2021-05-09; es); passages p0012, p0014, p0015, p0016, p0044. [Structured record](../../records/src-3cfcc923505e.json).

Related topics: [Perceptual density](perceptual-density.md).

### src-3cfcc923505e-c02

Yazhi says repeating lives is neither inherently positive nor negative; its value depends on whether the experience serves the soul. She says the person in the loop judges its value.

Attributed to **Yazhi**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Reencarnación - Bucles de Alma - Información Extraterrestre de Yazhi Swaruu](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/reencarnacion-bucles-de-alma-informacion-extraterrestre-de-yazhi-swaruu) (2021-05-09; es); passages p0033, p0034. [Structured record](../../records/src-3cfcc923505e.json).
