# Protective charms

Type: technology

Aliases: amulets, iron charms

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-70151b61ae43-c02

Yazhi attributes protection chiefly to focused intention and fearlessness; charms and crystals can help focus a person’s power. She distinguishes paranormal defense from ordinary physical threats.

Attributed to **Yazhi Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Protección Espiritual de Influencias Negativas y Entidades Astrales - Yazhi Swaruu](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/proteccion-espiritual-de-influencias-negativas-y-entidades-astrales-yazhi-swaruu) (2021-10-07; es); passages p0031, p0035, p0043, p0127. ### src-70151b61ae43-c04

Yazhi recommends iron, especially magnetite, as a protective charm and suggests placing iron at room corners. She calls magnetite the best option.

Attributed to **Yazhi Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Protección Espiritual de Influencias Negativas y Entidades Astrales - Yazhi Swaruu](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/proteccion-espiritual-de-influencias-negativas-y-entidades-astrales-yazhi-swaruu) (2021-10-07; es); passages p0147, p0152, p0154, p0156.
