# Lyrians

Type: civilization

Aliases: raza liriana, Lyrian race

## Collected claims

### src-7d76d15d2444-c02

She says some Federation groups genetically adapt Lyrian migrants’ bodies to their chosen planets, matching bodies to existing consciousness. She says she lacks specific details.

Attributed to **Swaruu X (Athena)**; reported; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Hibridación - Población de Nuevos Planetas - Athena Swaruu](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/hibridacion-poblacion-de-nuevos-planetas-athena-swaruu) (2023-04-07; es); passages p0021, p0038, p0046. [Structured record](../../records/src-7d76d15d2444.json).

Related topics: [DNA and metaphysical patterns](dna-metaphysics.md).

Source: [ESTÁN ALMACENANDO TU ADN – EXTRATERRESTRE ME HABLA DEL GENOMA HUMANO - Yazhi Swaruu](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/estan-almacenando-tu-adn-extraterrestre-me-habla-del-genoma-humano-yazhi-swaruu) (2022-12-13; es); passages p0033. [Structured record](../../records/src-ce97d34c532b.json).

### src-07f9f86e1027-c02

She said non-Earth human civilizations commonly trace human origins to planets around Vega in Lyra and associate their migration with the Great Expansion and Orion Wars. She added humans existed earlier and elsewhere too.

Attributed to **Mari Swaruu**; reported; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Orígenes de la Raza Lyriana / Humana](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/origenes-de-la-raza-lyriana-humana) (2022-12-16; es); passages p0015. [Structured record](../../records/src-07f9f86e1027.json).

### src-5699fcba6376-c03

Source: [¿Qué es un extraterrestre para ti?](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/que-es-un-extraterrestre-para-ti) (2023-05-18; es); passages p0031, p0032, p0033, p0034, p0035. [Structured record](../../records/src-5699fcba6376.json).
