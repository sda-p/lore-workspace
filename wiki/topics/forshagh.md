# Forshagh

Type: sport

Aliases: Urmah contact sport

## Collected claims

### src-9ce83f769cba-c01

Arishah describes forshagh as a violent Urmah sport: typically 12 players race from numbered goals to seize a 60-kilogram artificial prey and carry or drag it into their own goal.

Attributed to **Arishah**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Arishah, el Tigre Urmah, Parte 4.
