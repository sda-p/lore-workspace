# Scimitar-class craft

Type: technology

Aliases: Scimitar, Scimitar A, Scimitar B

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-8a37a5eaeb7b-c01

Anéeka says Scimitar A ships are light interceptors; B assault variants carry troops and up to two armored vehicles or a human-style helicopter. Athena says one pilot or onboard AI can fly either.

Attributed to **Anéeka; Swaruu X (Athena)**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [NAVES EXTRATERRESTRES PUEDEN ENVIAR MENSAJES DE LUZ EN FORMA DE DESTELLOS](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/naves-extraterrestres-pueden-enviar-mensajes-de-luz-en-forma-de-destellos) (2023-07-09; es); passages p0016, p0033, p0034, p0036. [Structured record](../../records/src-8a37a5eaeb7b.json).

### src-4e79a5644fae-c01

Dhor says Rag disabled the Scimitar’s shields during an electric snowstorm to avoid glowing near military sensors; lightning struck the craft. Yazhi later confirms shields were off to avoid detection.

Attributed to **Dhor Káal’el**; reported; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Vida Interestelar 9 - Nave espacial alcanzada por un Rayo - Explicación de la Misión de 2021](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/interstellar-life-9-spacecraft-struck-by-lightning-2021-mission-explained) (2025-05-09; es); passages p0025, p0026, p0031, p0037. [Structured record](../../records/src-4e79a5644fae.json).

Related topics: [Starship systems](starship-systems.md).

### src-4e79a5644fae-c02

Dhor says lightning entered through a non-polymorphic antenna, damaged communications and cut muon contact; superconducting cables carried excess charge to engine plasma, but damaged circuits needed replacement. The hull self-repaired, but antenna and electronics did not.

Attributed to **Dhor Káal’el**; reported; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Vida Interestelar 9 - Nave espacial alcanzada por un Rayo - Explicación de la Misión de 2021](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/interstellar-life-9-spacecraft-struck-by-lightning-2021-mission-explained) (2025-05-09; es); passages p0028, p0029, p0033.
