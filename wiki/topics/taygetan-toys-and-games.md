# Taygetan toys and games

Type: culture

Aliases: juguetes y juegos en Taygeta

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-f2e40d010e31-c01

Yazhi says Taygetans make toys from durable, moldable non-hydrocarbon resin; children often build them by hand or design and replicate them at home. She says toy preferences vary and there are no gender-role restrictions.

Attributed to **Yazhi**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Juguetes y Juegos en Taygeta - Yazhi y Raguel de Temmer \*\*SIN VIDEO\*\*](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/juguetes-y-juegos-en-taygeta-yazhi-y-raguel-de-temmer) (2021-05-03; es); passages p0003, p0005, p0006. [Structured record](../../records/src-f2e40d010e31.json).

### src-f2e40d010e31-c02

Raguel describes a cooperative Taygetan building-design board game requiring memory, spatial planning, and usually telepathic sharing of detailed mental images. She says spoken descriptions can substitute for telepathy.

Attributed to **Raguel**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Juguetes y Juegos en Taygeta - Yazhi y Raguel de Temmer \*\*SIN VIDEO\*\*](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/juguetes-y-juegos-en-taygeta-yazhi-y-raguel-de-temmer) (2021-05-03; es); passages p0013, p0016, p0018. [Structured record](../../records/src-f2e40d010e31.json).
