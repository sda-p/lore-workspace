# Taygetan Hanáre/Scimitar fighters

Type: starship class

Aliases: Hanáre, Scimitar

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-aa0d46c03756-c01

Swaruu X (Athena) describes Hanáre/Scimitar A as an interceptor and B as an assault and evacuation craft carrying troops and vehicles. The A is faster; the B is heavier.

Attributed to **Swaruu X (Athena)**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [NAVES ESTELARES EXTRATERRESTRES TAYGETEANAS](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/naves-estelares-extraterrestres-taygeteanas) (2023-07-07; es); passages p0008, p0010, p0011, p0016, p0018, p0019, p0022. [Structured record](../../records/src-aa0d46c03756.json).

Related topics: [Starship systems](starship-systems.md), [Taygetans](taygetans.md).

### src-aa0d46c03756-c02

Athena says toroidal high-energy stealth hides Scimitars from human visual, infrared, and microwave detection. She says gravitic-shift detectors could still detect them.

Attributed to **Swaruu X (Athena)**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [NAVES ESTELARES EXTRATERRESTRES TAYGETEANAS](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/naves-estelares-extraterrestres-taygeteanas) (2023-07-07; es); passages p0032, p0034. [Structured record](../../records/src-aa0d46c03756.json).

Related topics: [Starship systems](starship-systems.md).

### src-aa0d46c03756-c03

Athena describes older Scimitars as reliable and durable, though their engine components are difficult to access and maintain. Despite age and maintenance demands, she says they remain in service.

Attributed to **Swaruu X (Athena)**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [NAVES ESTELARES EXTRATERRESTRES TAYGETEANAS](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/naves-estelares-extraterrestres-taygeteanas) (2023-07-07; es); passages p0042, p0043, p0044. [Structured record](../../records/src-aa0d46c03756.json).
