# Shadow People

Type: species

Aliases: shadow people, shadow figures

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-852d7521088f-c01

Mari says biological, humanlike Shadow People come from Mensa and exist three-quarters in the lower astral, one-quarter physical, causing their ghostlike appearance. Mari presents this as information from her group.

Attributed to **Mari Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [La Gente Sombra es una raza extraterrestre.](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/la-gente-sombra-es-una-raza-extraterrestre) (2023-05-21; es); passages p0014, p0015, p0016. [Structured record](../../records/src-852d7521088f.json).

Related topics: [Alien species and distinctions](alien-species.md).

### src-852d7521088f-c02

Mari says the Federation classifies them as low-danger regressives outside its membership; they are interstellar and often invade less advanced cultures. Attributed to Mari’s account.

Attributed to **Mari Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [La Gente Sombra es una raza extraterrestre.](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/la-gente-sombra-es-una-raza-extraterrestre) (2023-05-21; es); passages p0016, p0017, p0018. [Structured record](../../records/src-852d7521088f.json).

Related topics: [Galactic Federation](galactic-federation.md).

### src-d522bad591d5-c01

Yazhi describes shadow people as semi-physical, semi-astral beings rather than egregors, with most existence in the astral and some in the physical. She offers this as her best explanation.

Attributed to **Yazhi Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Gente Sombra - Charla Complementaria con Yazhi sobre el video de Mari Swaruu](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/gente-sombra-charla-complementaria-con-yazhi-sobre-el-video-de-mari-swaruu) (2023-05-26; es); passages p0022, p0032, p0046, p0048.
