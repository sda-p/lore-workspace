# Replicators

Type: technology

Aliases: matter replicators, industrial replicators

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-5ed98072b8ce-c01

Mari Swaruu says advanced Taygetan replicators create matter from energy, but exact copies of unfamiliar complex objects still require an original to scan. She distinguishes exact replication from designing a similar-looking object.

Attributed to **Mari Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Tecnologia Taygeteana 02, replicadores industriales, ropa y maquillaje](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/tecnologia-taygeteana-02-replicadores-industriales-ropa-y-maquillaje) (2023-02-25; es); passages p0015, p0016, p0018, p0024. [Structured record](../../records/src-5ed98072b8ce.json).

Related topics: [Energy generation technology](energy-generation.md).

### src-5ed98072b8ce-c02

Mari Swaruu says Taygetan starships carry large engineering replicators that make replacement parts for engines and other systems, reducing dry-dock repairs. She says this gives the ships nearly unlimited range.

Attributed to **Mari Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Tecnologia Taygeteana 02, replicadores industriales, ropa y maquillaje](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/tecnologia-taygeteana-02-replicadores-industriales-ropa-y-maquillaje) (2023-02-25; es); passages p0023. [Structured record](../../records/src-5ed98072b8ce.json).
