### src-65d7f9508cf4-c03

She classifies Source-intended Lyrians and Taygetans as primary, but humans as a modified Lyran secondary species.

Source: [LA MATRIX - SWARUU - LAS PLÉYADES - TAYGETA](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/la-matrix-swaruu-las-pleyades-taygeta) (2018-06-30; es); passages p0020, p0021, p0022. [Structured record](../../records/src-65d7f9508cf4.json).

### src-4e74e1caea83-c01

Yazhi says humans, Taygetans, and Engans are Lyrian in origin, but humans have distinct brain and nervous-system development and lack telepathy and extrasensory perception. She says humans are considered secondary by some, while Andromedans reportedly regard their genetics as superior.

Attributed to **Yazhi**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Genoma Humano y Recolección del ADN - Yazhi Swaruu](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/genoma-humano-y-recoleccion-del-adn-yazhi-swaruu) (2023-03-30; es); passages p0031, p0032, p0033, p0034. [Structured record](../../records/src-4e74e1caea83.json).

### src-75919d9e49b8-c02

Anéeka says humans were not laboratory-created but comprise nested lineages of 22 races, with invasive genetic alterations reverting over generations without mind control. She describes the 22-race figure as a simplified account of a much larger nested ancestry.

Attributed to **Anéeka**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [LA ACTIVACIÓN DE ADN Y EL DESPERTAR DE LA HUMANIDAD – ATHENA SWARUU – ANEEKA DE TEMMER](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/la-activacion-de-adn-y-el-despertar-de-la-humanidad-athena-swaruu-aneeka-de-temmer) (2023-02-01; es); passages p0004, p0005.
