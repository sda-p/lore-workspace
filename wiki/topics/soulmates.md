# Soulmates

Type: cosmology

Aliases: soul mates, twin flames

These are source-specific assertions; disagreement is preserved rather than resolved by publication order.

## Collected claims

### src-30fbc888024d-c01

Swaruu describes soulmates as souls repeatedly drawn together, appearing as one soul from an expanded perspective.

Attributed to **Swaruu**; reported; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Soulmates - Do they Exist? Chat with Swaruu of Erra (Taygeta, Pleiades)](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/soulmates-do-they-exist-chat-with-swaruu-of-erra-taygeta-pleiades) (2021-11-21; en); passages p0008, p0010, p0011, p0016. [Structured record](../../records/src-30fbc888024d.json).
