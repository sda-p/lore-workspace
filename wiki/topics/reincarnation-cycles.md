# Reincarnation and karmic cycles

Type: cosmology

Aliases: ciclos de reencarnación, karma

## Collected claims

### src-6c81d51e9d7c-c01

Swaruu says Earth reincarnation can become a trap imposed by religions and an alleged Van Allen etheric barrier; she claims sufficiently high frequency may escape it.

Attributed to **Swaruu**; reported; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Swaruu y Reencarnacion: Mensaje Extraterrestre Pleyadiano (Taygeta) (19)](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/swaruu-y-reencarnacion-mensaje-extraterrestre-pleyadiano-taygeta-19) (2019-02-11; es); passages p0053. [Structured record](../../records/src-6c81d51e9d7c.json).

### src-6c81d51e9d7c-c02

She describes reincarnation spirals linking people who believe they owe one another karmic debts, sometimes as the same soul across lives.

Attributed to **Swaruu**; reported; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Swaruu y Reencarnacion: Mensaje Extraterrestre Pleyadiano (Taygeta) (19)](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/swaruu-y-reencarnacion-mensaje-extraterrestre-pleyadiano-taygeta-19) (2019-02-11; es); passages p0043, p0044, p0046, p0048. [Structured record](../../records/src-6c81d51e9d7c.json).

Source: [Swaruu y Reencarnacion: Mensaje Extraterrestre Pleyadiano (Taygeta) (19)](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/swaruu-y-reencarnacion-mensaje-extraterrestre-pleyadiano-taygeta-19) (2019-02-11; es); passages p0065, p0067, p0069. [Structured record](../../records/src-6c81d51e9d7c.json).
