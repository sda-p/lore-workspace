### src-e296ebcd3f46-c01

Mari Swaruu says Antarctica contains ancient Federation star bases, used for planetary control since the last Tiamat-related flood around 12,500 years ago. She says many bases are abandoned and some remain active.

Attributed to **Mari Swaruu**; reported; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Antártida, 01 - mi informe actual](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/antartida-01-mi-informe-actual) (2023-02-21; es); passages p0018. [Structured record](../../records/src-e296ebcd3f46.json).

### src-e296ebcd3f46-c02

Mari Swaruu describes Antarctic Federation bases as multispecies underground cities linked to other deep bases by high-speed magnetic trains. She says nonhuman visitors use Antarctica as an entry point before traveling underground.

Attributed to **Mari Swaruu**; asserted; extraction confidence: high.

Source: [Antártida, 01 - mi informe actual](https://swaruu.org/transcripts/antartida-01-mi-informe-actual) (2023-02-21; es); passages p0020, p0024, p0025. [Structured record](../../records/src-e296ebcd3f46.json).
